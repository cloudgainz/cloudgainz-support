Support

For help with your Cloudgainz subscription, email our support team.

How to reach us

support@cloudgainz.com

For sales questions, write to get@cloudgainz.com instead.

What to include

Include these details so we can find your subscription quickly:

  • Your Microsoft Marketplace subscription ID.
  • Your Microsoft Entra tenant ID.
  • What you were doing when the problem happened.
  • A screenshot, if you have one.

How fast we answer

We reply within one business day.

Manage or cancel a subscription

You manage and cancel this subscription through Microsoft, in the Azure portal under SaaS. Cancel within 72 hours of purchase and you are eligible for a full refund. After that, email us and we will look at your request.

The full rules are in Microsoft’s Refund policies for Microsoft Marketplace.
