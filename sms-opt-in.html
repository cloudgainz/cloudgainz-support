SMS Opt-In Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This page describes the text messages Cloudgainz LLC sends from its toll-free number +1 (888) 890-2784, who receives them, and how consent is collected.

1. What the messages are

Internal operational alerts. When a customer support email sent to Cloudgainz is close to its response deadline and has not been answered, our support system texts the Cloudgainz staff member on call so they answer it by email. A second alert says the support system itself needs attention. We send no marketing or promotional messages.

2. Who receives them

Cloudgainz employees only. No customers or members of the public are enrolled, and there is no public sign-up.

3. How consent is collected

Consent is verbal. A Cloudgainz administrator asks the employee, in person or by phone, using this script:

“Cloudgainz will text you at this mobile number when a customer support email is close to its response deadline, or when the support system needs attention. Expect up to 50 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive these texts?”

The employee gives their own mobile number only after agreeing. The administrator records the number, the date and the consent.

4. Sample messages

  • Cloudgainz: support reply due 3:00 PM ET, answer the email. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Cloudgainz: support agent needs attention, check email. Reply STOP to opt out.

5. Frequency, opt-out and help

Up to 50 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help, or email support@cloudgainz.com.

6. Privacy

Mobile numbers are used only for these alerts. They are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Messages never contain customer information. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.
